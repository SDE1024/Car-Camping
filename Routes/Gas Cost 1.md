Using **33 MPG** and the current AAA regular-gas averages for the states along each route:

- **1,477-mile trip** — Michigan → Indiana/Illinois → Iowa/Missouri/Kansas → Oklahoma. Those state averages work out to roughly **$4.17/gallon**. [AAA Fuel Prices](https://gasprices.aaa.com/state-gas-price-averages/?Sid=1) [AAA Fuel Prices](https://gasprices.aaa.com/state-gas-price-averages/?Sid=1)  
  **1,477 ÷ 33 × $4.17 ≈ $187**

- **2,131-mile trip** — Oklahoma → Texas/Louisiana/Mississippi/Tennessee/Alabama/Georgia → Florida. Average regular is roughly **$3.97/gallon**. [AAA Fuel Prices](https://gasprices.aaa.com/state-gas-price-averages/?Sid=1) [AAA Fuel Prices](https://gasprices.aaa.com/state-gas-price-averages/?Sid=1) [AAA Fuel Prices](https://gasprices.aaa.com/state-gas-price-averages/?Sid=1)  
  **2,131 ÷ 33 × $3.97 ≈ $257**

**Combined fuel cost: about $444.**

This should be more realistic than using the nationwide gas average because most of the **2,131-mile southern route has cheaper gas**.
